#### Why I built it

Every morning I was opening four apps to figure out my day. So I built one screen that pulls in my Todoist tasks, Google Calendar, the Gmail threads that actually need a reply, and the weather, with a one-line AI summary on top.

#### How

Next.js and TypeScript, built end to end with Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex, across five phases I scoped one at a time. The AI summary is the newest piece. I review every AI-suggested change before it goes in, the same way I checked AI-drafted code at EchoStar.

#### Why it matters to me

The teams I want to PM for are building with AI every day. I wanted to know what that actually feels like from the inside, not just talk about it.
