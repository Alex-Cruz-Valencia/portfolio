#### What I do

I mentor two new PMs, each leading a different DALI Lab team. One is building an AI learning platform that creates personalized practice for students and shows instructors how their class is doing. The other is working on two games projects with a campus partner: a community story experience and an arcade-style game picker.

#### How

I'm not building their products. I help them write requirements, make prioritization calls, plan user research, and run stakeholder meetings. I also taught a 4-session PM course to about 15 students at the lab.

#### What I've learned

Explaining why you'd cut a feature is the fastest way to find out whether you actually know.
