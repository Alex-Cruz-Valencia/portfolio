#### The idea

Dartmouth Football's coaching staff needed a better way to keep track of players and scouting. I lead a four-person team through the Dartmouth Society of Computer Science Students that's building that platform with them.

#### How we work

The coaches are both our users and our stakeholders, so the work starts in conversations with them. My job is turning what they need into requirements and a priority order my team can actually build from.

#### What we've built so far

Player profiles and planning tools came first, because everything else depends on them. We've cut the scope down to what matters most to the staff, and we keep reworking mockups and workflows every time coaches react to them. It's the closest thing I've had to a client project, and it's still going.
