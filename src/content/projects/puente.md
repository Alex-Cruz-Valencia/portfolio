#### The idea

A lot of immigrant parents want to learn English, and a lot of their kids are already fluent. Puente, Spanish for "bridge," would turn that into a structured app where the kid is the guide, with nonprofits that serve immigrant families as the way in.

#### Why I'm not building it yet

Three assumptions hold the whole thing up, and any of them could be wrong:

1. **The barrier is a missing tool.** It might really be time, cost, childcare, or embarrassment. If parents already have free apps they never open, another app won't fix that.
2. **Kids want the teaching role.** A teenager correcting a parent every night flips a family dynamic, and some kids will hate it.
3. **Nonprofits will commit.** Encouragement is free. An introduction, a room, or a pilot is the real signal.

#### The plan

Nonprofits first, because one person who works with dozens of families can tell me in an hour what six parent interviews would take a month to surface. Then six to eight parents and three or four kids, talked to separately, since a teenager won't say "this is embarrassing" with their mom in the room. I wrote down what I expect to hear before any of it, so I can tell what actually surprises me.

#### What counts as a win

A commitment, not enthusiasm. And if the idea breaks, that's still the answer I need. I'd rather kill my own idea for clear reasons than build something nobody tested.
