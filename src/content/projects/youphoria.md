#### The role

Youphoria is a social wellness and fitness app, live on the App Store. I came in through a general internship and was made Product Director within a month. From then on, my job was deciding which new ideas were worth building, in what order, and how, then testing them before they shipped.

#### Groups

I had to argue for this one. What made Youphoria different was how much it leaned into the social side of wellness, and Groups fit that: a group chat, a shared goal (like walking a certain amount together), and a way to track and cheer on each other's progress. My bet was that people stick with habits when a group is counting on them. I vetted it, sequenced it, oversaw the build, and tested it. Groups launched, and most of our testers used it, with encouraging retention. Results were mixed across cohorts, so I'd call it a strong signal, not a clean lift.

#### The backlog

Requests came in from everywhere: users, teammates, outside contributors. I triaged them into what had to happen now and what could wait. A couple of the fixes that shipped: a "verify" button nobody understood (how do you verify someone's solo workout?) and a home screen that was way too cluttered.
