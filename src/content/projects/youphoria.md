#### The role

Youphoria is a social wellness and fitness app, live on the App Store with 500+ users and a team of about 50. I came in through a general internship and was made Product Director within a month. From then on, my job was deciding which new ideas were worth building, in what order, and how, then testing them before they shipped.

#### Groups

I had to argue for this one. Users were dropping off sharply after day 3. What made Youphoria different was how much it leaned into the social side of wellness, and Groups fit that: a group chat, a shared goal (like walking a certain amount together), and a way to track and cheer on each other's progress. My bet was that people stick with habits when a group is counting on them. I vetted it, sequenced it, oversaw the build, and tested it. Groups launched, and 17 of the 23 testers we enrolled used it actively. By my estimate from our tester data, group members came back on day 7 about 70% more often. It's a small sample and results varied by cohort, so I'd call it a strong signal, not a clean lift.

#### Getting it out there

I also partnered with marketing on launch pushes, defining who we were talking to and what we said to them.

#### The backlog

Requests came in from everywhere: users, teammates, outside contributors. I triaged them into what had to happen now and what could wait. A couple of the fixes that shipped: a "verify" button nobody understood (how do you verify someone's solo workout?) and a home screen that was way too cluttered.
